Neck & Shoulder Pain Fort McMurray

Home › Neck & Shoulder Pain

Long hours of desk work, driving, heavy lifting, and chronic stress often culminate into the same problem: relentless neck and shoulder tension. When standard treatments only provide temporary relief, it's time to look deeper at the structural connections.

Symptoms We Commonly Treat

  • Stiff neck that won't turn fully or comfortably
  • Shoulder blade tightness, knotting, and deep aching
  • Headaches that originate at the base of the skull and wrap over the head
  • Jaw tension and clicking (TMJ issues)
  • Arm tingling, numbness, or "heavy" sensations
  • Pain that consistently returns exactly the same way after massage or standard physio

The Connection Between Neck, Jaw & Shoulder

The body works as a kinetic chain, which means pain in one area is often driven by restriction in another. A restricted upper thoracic spine (the upper back) will force the cervical spine (neck) to hyper-mobilize to keep your eyes level. A clenched jaw pulls constantly on the temporalis and masseter muscles, referring tension down the front and sides of the neck. When the shoulder blade doesn't track smoothly over the ribcage, the trapezius and levator scapula muscles have to overwork just to lift your arm.

This web of compensations is why rubbing a sore shoulder or stretching a tight neck feels good for a day, but rarely fixes the problem. The muscles aren't the problem — they are the victims of poor mechanics.

Treatment Approach

We conduct a whole-body assessment to trace the kinetic chain and identify the real driver of your pain. Are your ribs restricted? Is the clavicle misaligned? Is the jaw causing a downward pull? Once the source is located, precise osteopathic manual techniques are used to restore joint geometry, decompress nerves, and release fibrotic tissues, allowing the muscles to finally relax on their own.
