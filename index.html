The Bodyfix99 Approach
The Art of Transformation

Assessment Before Action

Every session starts with understanding your body as a whole, mapping the compensation patterns before any treatment begins.

Precision Over Pressure

Targeted clinical techniques replace forceful treatments. We work with the nervous system rather than forcing tissues to release.

From Compensation to Balance

Releasing the patterns your body uses to cope, resetting your structural alignment so muscles can unguard naturally.

Restoring Function First

Pain reduction reliably follows improved function. By fixing how the joint operates, the surrounding pain dissipates.

Treating the System Not the Spot

The source of pain is often far from where you feel it. We trace the kinetic chain to find the actual structural driver.

Long-Term Change

The goal is lasting transformation in how your body works, significantly reducing your dependency on manual therapy over time.
